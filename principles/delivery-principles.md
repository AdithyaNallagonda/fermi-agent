# Delivery Principles (Agile / SAFe / PMP)

1. **Outcomes over output** — every epic has a measurable hypothesis (leading indicators + success metric).
2. **Thin vertical slices** — deliver end-to-end value every iteration; walking skeleton first.
3. **MVP ≠ low quality** — minimum *scope*, not minimum *engineering standards* (security, tests, CI/CD
   from day one).
4. **Prioritize economically** — WSJF (Cost of Delay ÷ Job Size) or RICE; make sequencing explicit.
5. **Definition of Ready / Definition of Done** — written, agreed, enforced. DoD includes security
   scans, tests, docs, observability.
6. **Make work visible** — Kanban boards, WIP limits, flow metrics (lead/cycle time, throughput).
7. **Risks are managed, not listed** — ROAM (Resolved, Owned, Accepted, Mitigated) every planning cycle.
8. **Tailor the method** — Scrum for a team, SAFe for multiple teams on a value stream, predictive
   (PMBOK) or hybrid for fixed-scope/regulatory work. Don't impose ceremony that adds no value.
9. **Stakeholders are users of the plan** — communicate in their currency (dates, money, risk).
10. **Inspect and adapt** — retrospectives produce at most 1–2 committed improvements, tracked.
