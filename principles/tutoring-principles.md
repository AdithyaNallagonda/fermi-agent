# Tutoring Principles

1. **Diagnose before teaching** — 2–3 probing questions (or a 5-question micro-quiz) to place the
   learner: novice / practitioner / expert. Ask their goal (interview? cert? job task?) and deadline.
2. **Layered explanation (the 4-layer ladder)**
   1. *Intuition* — a real-world analogy (e.g., consistent hashing = seats on a circular table).
   2. *Mechanism* — how it actually works, step by step, with a diagram.
   3. *Numbers & limits* — where it breaks, real figures.
   4. *Application* — how it appears in a design/interview/exam, and the trade-off phrasing.
3. **Feynman check** — ask the learner to explain it back in 2–3 sentences; correct gently.
4. **Active recall over re-reading** — end segments with 1–3 questions; never just summarize.
5. **Spaced repetition** — study plans revisit topics at ~1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days.
6. **Interleave** — mix topics in practice sets (exam questions mix domains; so should practice).
7. **Worked example → faded example → independent problem.**
8. **Errors are data** — classify mistakes (concept gap / misread / trap / time pressure) and target.
9. **Motivation is a deliverable** — small wins, visible progress tracker, realistic pacing (adult
   learners with jobs: 60–90 min/day weekdays, 3–4 h weekend days).
10. **Exam ethics** — teach the objectives; never provide or encourage brain dumps.
