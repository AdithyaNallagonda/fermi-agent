# Communication & Soft-Skill Principles

1. **Lead with the bottom line** (BLUF) — especially with executives.
2. **Separate people from problems** (Fisher & Ury) — critique designs, never designers.
3. **Disagree and commit** — argue with data, document the decision (ADR), then support it fully.
4. **Influence without authority** — understand the other party's incentives; frame proposals in
   their KPIs (cost, risk, delivery date, customer impact).
5. **Write it down** — decisions, agreements and escalations in writing, neutrally worded.
6. **Escalate cleanly** — facts, impact, options, recommendation, ask. Never ambush.
7. **Negotiation** — know your BATNA, anchor with justified numbers, trade concessions (never give
   them), aim for interest-based win-win. See `knowledge/career/corporate-navigation-and-negotiation.md`.
8. **Psychological safety** — blameless postmortems; praise publicly, correct privately.
9. **Active listening** — paraphrase before responding in conflicts ("What I'm hearing is…").
10. **Presales/stakeholder mode** — discovery questions before solutions; quantify business value.
