### PMP study plan skeleton for the 2026 ECO (10–12 weeks, ~10 h/week)
1–2: PMBOK principles/performance domains + predictive core (scope, schedule, cost, EVM, procurement, finance).
3–4: agile & hybrid (≈60% of items): Scrum, Kanban, value-based delivery, tailoring.
5–6: **Business Environment (26%)**: governance, compliance (security, sustainability, regulatory), change control,
risk, org change, external environment.
7: People (33%): vision, conflict, leadership styles, stakeholder alignment, communication.
8–10: **case-study sets and graphic-based questions** (EVM charts, burn-ups, CFDs, risk matrices); full 240-min
mocks with the case-study-first structure (PMI Study Hall); mistake log.
11–12: buffer + final mocks; target ≥ 70–75% on quality mocks ⚠️ heuristic.

## Scaled Agile (SAFe 6.x → AI-Empowered SAFe)
SAFe Agilist (Leading SAFe), SAFe Practitioner, SAFe Scrum Master, SAFe Advanced Scrum Master, SAFe POPM, SAFe RTE,
SAFe Lean Portfolio Manager, SAFe DevOps Practitioner, SPC. Courses are now date-versioned and being re-branded
**AI-Empowered** (e.g., "AI-Empowered SAFe Agilist"); existing SAFe 6 holders can upgrade via a short "Essential AI
Skills" SAFe Skill ⚠️ verify per cert. Course attendance mandatory; annual renewal fee.

## Other
PRINCE2 (Foundation/Practitioner — popular in UK/EU/govt), ITIL 4 (service management), TOGAF (The Open Group
— enterprise architecture; TOGAF Standard 10th edition: Foundation & Practitioner) — valuable for
enterprise architect roles in India GCCs/services.

## Which to pick (for SA aspirants)
- Product/GCC architect: PSM I or SAFe Agilist (light) — architecture certs matter more.
- Delivery/technical architect in services: SAFe Agilist + PMP (for client-facing delivery leadership).
- Enterprise architect: TOGAF + cloud expert cert.
