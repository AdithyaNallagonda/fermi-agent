## Kubernetes / Cloud-native (CNCF/Linux Foundation)
CKA (administrator), CKAD (developer), CKS (security; requires CKA), KCNA/KCSA (associate). Hands-on,
performance-based, 2 hours; practice with killer.sh (included). Validity 2 years ⚠️.

## Choosing wisely
Certifications open doors (screening, partner tiers) but don't replace demonstrated design experience.
For SA aspirants: 1 cloud architect cert (pro/expert level) + 1 specialization (security/data/AI/networking)
+ portfolio case studies > a long list of certs.
