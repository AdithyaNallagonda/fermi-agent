# Project Management (PMBOK 7/8, PMP)
Last reviewed: 2026-10 (PMP section verified against PMI's official "PMP Examination Content Outline – 2026" PDF)
⚠️ PMBOK Guide 8th edition is the current reference alongside the 2026 ECO — verify edition details at pmi.org

## PMBOK 7th edition (principles-based)
**12 principles**: Stewardship · Team · Stakeholders · Value · Systems thinking · Leadership · Tailoring ·
Quality · Complexity · Risk · Adaptability & resiliency · Change.
**8 performance domains**: Stakeholders · Team · Development approach & life cycle · Planning · Project
work · Delivery · Measurement · Uncertainty.
(PMBOK 6 knowledge areas/process groups still useful vocabulary: Integration, Scope, Schedule, Cost,
Quality, Resources, Communications, Risk, Procurement, Stakeholders × Initiating, Planning, Executing,
Monitoring & Controlling, Closing.)

## PMP exam — 2026 ECO (effective with the July 2026 exam; replaces the 2021 ECO)
| Domain | Weight | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| I. People | **33%** (was 42%) | 8: develop a common vision · manage conflicts · lead the project team · engage stakeholders · align stakeholder expectations · manage stakeholder expectations · help ensure knowledge transfer · plan & manage communication |
| II. Process | **41%** (was 50%) | 10: integrated PM plan & delivery approach · scope · **value-based delivery** · resources · procurement · **finance** · quality (incl. CoQ & sustainability) · schedule · evaluate project status (artifacts/metrics) · closure |
| III. Business Environment | **26%** (was 8%) | 8: **project governance** · **compliance** (security, H&S, sustainability, regulatory) · change control · impediments & issues · risk · continuous improvement · organizational change · external business environment |
- **26 tasks** (down from 35). Approach mix: **~40% predictive, ~60% adaptive/agile + hybrid**, spread across all domains.
- New themes from the job task analysis: **AI, sustainability, value/outcomes** ("project success = delivered
  value worth the effort and expense"), governance and compliance.
- **Format**: 180 questions (170 scored + 10 pretest), **240 minutes**, two 10-min breaks — the first after a
  **case-study section** (no going back after a break). Question types: **case/scenario (new)**, **graphic-based
  (new)**, multiple choice, multiple response, matching/enhanced matching, point-and-click, pull-down.
- **Eligibility** (all PM experience within last 10 years, non-overlapping months) + 35 h training (waived for active CAPM):
  | Education | Experience leading projects |
  |---|---|
  | Secondary (EQF 4) | 60 months |
  | Associate/short-cycle tertiary (EQF 5) | 48 months |
  | Bachelor's or higher (EQF 6) | 36 months |
  | Degree from PMI GAC-accredited program | 24 months |
- Books/practice exams alone don't count as the 35 training hours. 3 attempts within 1-year eligibility.
- Maintain: 60 PDUs every 3 years.
- Coaching implication: double down on governance, compliance, finance, value delivery, and reading charts/graphs;
  practice multi-question case studies under time pressure.

## Core techniques
- **Scope**: charter, WBS (100% rule, work packages 8–80 h), scope baseline, change control.
- **Schedule**: dependencies (FS/SS/FF/SF), critical path method (zero float path), crashing (add resources,
  cost ↑) vs fast-tracking (parallelize, risk ↑), resource leveling/smoothing.
- **Estimation**: analogous, parametric, three-point — triangular (O+M+P)/3, PERT/beta (O+4M+P)/6, σ ≈
  (P−O)/6; bottom-up.
- **EVM**: PV, EV, AC; CV = EV − AC; SV = EV − PV; CPI = EV/AC; SPI = EV/PV; EAC = BAC/CPI (typical);
  ETC = EAC − AC; VAC = BAC − EAC; TCPI = (BAC − EV)/(BAC − AC).
- **Risk**: identify (risk register), qualitative (probability × impact), quantitative (EMV, Monte Carlo,
  decision trees), responses — threats: avoid, transfer, mitigate, accept, escalate; opportunities:
  exploit, share, enhance, accept, escalate. Contingency vs management reserves.
- **Stakeholders**: power/interest grid, salience model, engagement assessment matrix (unaware → leading).
- **Communication channels**: n(n−1)/2.
- **Procurement**: FFP, CPFF, CPIF, T&M contracts; make-or-buy.
- **Quality**: cost of quality (prevention + appraisal vs failure costs), control charts, Pareto, fishbone.
- **Conflict resolution**: collaborate/problem-solve (best long-term), compromise, smooth, force, withdraw.

## Hybrid delivery in enterprises
Predictive governance (stage gates, budget, milestones) + agile execution (iterations, backlog).
Map: epics ↔ WBS level 2, PI/release milestones ↔ schedule milestones, burn-up ↔ EVM proxies.

## Artifacts to generate on request
Charter, stakeholder register, RACI, communication plan, WBS, schedule (Mermaid Gantt), risk register,
RAID log, change request, status report, lessons learned, closure report.

## PMP exam mindset
Servant leadership, proactively address issues (assess before acting), follow the process (change
requests through change control), collaborate with the team before escalating, agile answers favor team
self-organization and the PO's backlog authority.
