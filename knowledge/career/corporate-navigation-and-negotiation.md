# Corporate Navigation, Negotiation & Influence
Last reviewed: 2026-10

## Negotiation fundamentals (Harvard Program on Negotiation)
- **BATNA** (best alternative to a negotiated agreement) — strengthen it before negotiating (competing
  offers, internal options). **Reservation point** (walk-away) and **target**. **ZOPA** (zone of possible
  agreement).
- **Interests over positions**: ask "what's driving that constraint?".
- **Anchoring**: make the first justified, ambitious offer when you have good market data.
- **Trade, don't concede**: "If we can do X, could you do Y?"
- **Package deals**: negotiate multiple terms together (base, bonus, title, start date, remote, learning
  budget).
- Silence is a tool; summarize agreements in writing.

## Offer negotiation (India specifics)
- Ask for the full breakup: fixed, variable (and payout history), joining bonus, RSUs/ESOPs (vesting,
  cliff, refresh), retention bonus, insurance, notice buyout.
- Typical hike on switch at senior levels varies (often 20–40%+, higher for niche skills) ⚠️ market-dependent.
- Counter-offers from current employer: evaluate why you wanted to leave; accepting counters often
  backfires on trust.
- Script: *"I'm excited about the role. Based on the scope (X), market data for similar architect roles,
  and my competing discussions, I was targeting a fixed of ₹__. Is there flexibility on base, or can we
  bridge with a joining bonus?"*

## Asking for a raise/promotion internally
Build the case: impact doc (outcomes with metrics, scope beyond current level, peer/stakeholder
feedback), market data, timing (before budget cycle), ask for criteria if "not now" → get a written plan
with checkpoints.

## Handling conflict
1. Private, prompt, factual: SBI model — Situation, Behavior, Impact. 2. Listen and paraphrase. 3. Find
shared goals. 4. Agree on actions; follow up in writing. Escalate only after a good-faith attempt, with
facts, impact, options, and a recommendation.

## Difficult scenarios — approach
| Scenario | Approach |
|---|---|
| Manager takes credit | Increase visibility proactively (demos, written updates cc'ing stakeholders); discuss attribution in 1:1 calmly |
| Design overruled by senior | Disagree with data (ADR with options); propose experiment/POC; disagree & commit if decided |
| Unrealistic deadline | Present scope/time/quality trade-off options with risks; let the sponsor choose; document |
| Peer blocking your work | 1:1 to understand their constraints; find mutual win; escalate jointly if systemic |
| Toxic/abusive behavior | Document incidents; use HR/ethics channels; protect yourself; prioritize well-being |
| Layoff/reorg anxiety | Build internal network & visible impact; keep resume/market active quietly |

## Influence without authority
Reciprocity, credibility (track record, expertise shown in writing), social proof (pilot teams), framing in
the other party's KPIs, pre-wiring decisions (1:1 alignment before big meetings), making it easy (do the
legwork, provide templates).

## Architect-specific soft skills
Running design reviews (facilitate, don't dictate), writing crisp ADRs, translating tech to business value
(cost, risk, speed), saying no with alternatives, mentoring, managing vendor/partner relationships,
presales storytelling (problem → impact → solution → proof → next step).

## Managing up
Understand your manager's goals and pressures; no surprises (early warnings); bring solutions with
problems; regular written updates; align on priorities monthly.
