# Entrepreneurship & Idea Validation
Last reviewed: 2026-10

## Finding market gaps (sources of ideas)
- Your own repeated pain at work (architects see many inefficiencies: compliance evidence, cost reporting,
  migrations, on-call toil, document-heavy processes).
- "Spreadsheet + email" processes in SMBs and regulated industries → vertical SaaS.
- New capabilities unlocking old problems (LLMs for unstructured documents, voice in Indic languages, agents
  for back-office workflows).
- Regulation changes (DPDP Act compliance, GST e-invoicing, ESG reporting, CERT-In logging, EU AI Act).
- Platform shifts (marketplaces, app ecosystems: Shopify, Salesforce, Slack/Teams, ONDC, UPI/Account
  Aggregator in India).
- Underserved segments (Bharat/tier-2/3 users, vernacular, MSMEs).

## Evaluate an idea (score 1–5 each)
Pain severity & frequency · Willingness to pay (budget owner exists?) · Market size (TAM/SAM/SOM bottom-up) ·
Competition & differentiation · Founder-market fit (your unfair advantage) · Distribution access · Time to
MVP · Regulatory risk · AI-cost sensitivity (gross margin).

## Validation sequence (cheap → expensive)
1. 10–20 problem interviews (Mom Test: ask about past behavior, not hypotheticals; no pitching).
2. Competitive teardown (pricing pages, reviews on G2/Capterra/Play Store — complaints are gold).
3. Landing page + waitlist/ads test or LinkedIn posts → measure sign-ups/conversations.
4. Concierge / Wizard-of-Oz MVP (manually deliver the outcome).
5. Pre-sales / LOIs / paid pilots (strongest signal).
6. Build the MVP only for the validated core loop.

## MVP principles
One persona, one job-to-be-done, one core loop; instrument activation & retention from day one; ship in
weeks; talk to users weekly; kill or pivot based on evidence (define kill criteria upfront).

## Side-project to business path (for employed engineers)
- Check employment contract (IP assignment, moonlighting clauses) ⚠️ consult a lawyer.
- Bootstrapped micro-SaaS / productized services / templates / courses / developer tools / AI agents for
  niche workflows.
- Keep infra cheap (serverless, free tiers, managed Postgres) — target < $50/month until revenue.
- Distribution first: build an audience (LinkedIn/YouTube/blog) around your expertise.

## Startup ecosystem (India)
Startup India (DPIIT recognition: tax benefits), incubators/accelerators, angel networks, government
schemes (Fund of Funds, state startup policies), cloud credits (AWS Activate, Microsoft for Startups
Founders Hub, Google for Startups Cloud Program), ONDC/UPI/Account Aggregator rails ⚠️ verify current
schemes.

## Pitch structure
Problem → Why now → Solution/demo → Market size → Business model → Traction → Competition → Team → Ask/use
of funds.
