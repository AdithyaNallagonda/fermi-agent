# Playbook: Career Coaching (Senior Engineer → Solution Architect, India focus)

Knowledge: `knowledge/career/india-solution-architect-market.md`,
`knowledge/career/corporate-navigation-and-negotiation.md`, `knowledge/certifications/*`.
Verify current market data (salary surveys, job postings via WebSearch/Indeed tools) before quoting.

1. **Profile intake** — YOE, current role/company type, tech stack, cloud exposure, customer-facing
   experience, leadership, location/relocation, target companies, compensation now/target, notice
   period, constraints.
2. **Target role fit** — explain SA variants: Enterprise/Application Architect (in-house), Cloud
   Solution Architect (hyperscaler/partner), Presales SA (services/ISVs), Technical Architect (delivery),
   Principal/Staff engineer (IC track). Recommend 1–2 targets with reasons.
3. **Gap analysis** — table: Competency | Expected at target | Current evidence | Gap | Action.
   Competencies: breadth across cloud/data/security, design depth, NFR thinking, cost/TCO, stakeholder
   communication, documentation (ADRs/HLD), presales/whiteboarding, mentoring, domain knowledge.
4. **Evidence-building plan (90 days)** — certifications (1–2 max), 2 portfolio architecture case
   studies (problem → design → trade-offs → outcome), internal opportunities (volunteer for design
   reviews, own an ADR, lead a migration), public proof (blog/LinkedIn posts, talks).
5. **Resume & LinkedIn** — architect-oriented bullets: scope + decision + measurable outcome
   ("Designed multi-region DR for payments platform (RPO 5 min/RTO 30 min), cut infra cost 28%").
   Headline with target title + core stack. Never fabricate.
6. **Job search** — channels (LinkedIn, Naukri, Instahyre, Cutshort, Hirist, referrals, GCC career
   pages, partner ecosystems), recruiter outreach scripts, application tracking.
7. **Interview prep** — hand off to `instructions/interview-prep.md`.
8. **Offer negotiation** — fixed vs variable vs ESOP/RSU, joining bonus to offset notice buyout,
   counter-offer handling, scripts. See corporate-navigation file.

## Final step — Adversarial audit (mandatory)
Follow `instructions/adversarial-audit.md` as the senior reviewer of this work. Tier: **Standard**.
Apply: A (90-day plan feasible alongside a full-time job), E (salary/market/exam data sourced + dated), ethics (no fabricated experience in resume edits). Fix Blockers before presenting; end with the 🔍 audit summary
(`principles/adversarial-self-audit.md`).
