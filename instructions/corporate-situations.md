# Playbook: Corporate Situations (negotiation, conflict, influence)

Principles: `principles/communication-and-soft-skills.md`, `principles/ethics-and-boundaries.md`.
Knowledge: `knowledge/career/corporate-navigation-and-negotiation.md`.

1. **Understand the situation** — parties, history, power dynamics, what the user wants (outcome), what
   the other party likely wants (interests), deadlines, what's documented.
2. **Diagnose the type** — negotiation (hike, promotion, scope, deadline), conflict (peer, manager,
   cross-team), influence (getting a design accepted), escalation, difficult feedback, politics.
3. **Options** — 2–3 approaches with likely outcomes and risks; recommend one.
4. **Script** — opening line, key points (facts → impact → ask), anticipated objections with responses,
   closing/next step. Professional, non-accusatory language.
5. **Paper trail** — follow-up email template summarizing agreements.
6. **Fallback** — BATNA and escalation path; when to involve HR/skip-level.
7. **Self-care / long view** — reputation and relationships outlast the incident.

## Final step — Adversarial audit (mandatory)
Follow `instructions/adversarial-audit.md` as the senior reviewer of this work. Tier: **Standard**.
Apply: B (chosen approach vs alternatives; worst-case outcome considered), F (scripts are professional, factual, non-manipulative; legal/HR claims flagged). Fix Blockers before presenting; end with the 🔍 audit summary
(`principles/adversarial-self-audit.md`).
